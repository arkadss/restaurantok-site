Renia Bascov

Our terrace on Bascov beach, with the sunset right in front of you.

Since 1993

A story that began in a basement bar

Restaurant OK was founded in 1993 by Constantin Chirițescu and started out as a bar in the basement of the building. Today it is the oldest restaurant in Calafat, run by SC Sorlir Impex SRL, with a restaurant, a pastry shop and event rooms.
