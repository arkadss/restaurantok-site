Where you sit

The terrace, the room and the coffee corner

All three are at the same address, A.I. Cuza St. 26, in the town centre. Look for the “Gastro-pub OK” sign in front of the building.

Terasa OK

The main space of the restaurant: open air, with a retractable roof and shelter from the wind. No rain, no harsh sun, and a summer evening that lasts as long as you like.

TODO-poza: the Vidin Room set up

The Vidin Room

The old winter restaurant, today a room for groups of up to 35 people: anniversaries, memorial meals, company dinners, family gatherings. It can also be hired separately.

The front terrace

16–20 seats right on the street. From spring to autumn the morning sun warms it gently: the best spot for your morning coffee.

Cooked every morning

Three courses cooked that morning: soup, main course and a side. It changes every day and we announce it each morning on our Facebook page (in Romanian).

Today’s menu: call +4{{contact.telefon}} or write on WhatsApp. Eat in or take away.

Travelling through?

Calafat is at the Romanian end of the Calafat–Vidin bridge, on the DN56 road and the EuroVelo 6 cycling route. The restaurant is in the centre, a few minutes from the bridge. Prices are in lei, the Romanian currency.
