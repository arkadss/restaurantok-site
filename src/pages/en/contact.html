How to get here

From the bridge, from the road, from the river

Calafat is on the DN56 road, at the Romanian end of the Calafat–Vidin bridge over the Danube (Bulgaria is on the other bank), and on the EuroVelo 6 cycling route. The restaurant and the pastry shop are in the centre, on A.I. Cuza Street; the ballroom is on Traian Street, in the courtyard of the “Ștefan Milcu” high school.
